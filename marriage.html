The Sacrament of Holy Matrimony

What Is Christian Marriage?

Holy Matrimony is one of the seven Holy Mysteries of the Coptic Orthodox Church. Marriage is a divine institution established by God, through which a man and a woman are united in a sacred covenant of love, faithfulness, and lifelong commitment.

Christian marriage reflects the profound union between Christ and His Church, as described by St. Paul in Ephesians 5:31-32.

Marriage is more than a civil contract or social arrangement. It is a sacramental covenant blessed by God, through which the Holy Spirit grants grace to the husband and wife to grow together in holiness, love, and communion with Christ.

The husband and wife are called to support one another spiritually, practice sacrificial love, and establish a household centered on Christ.

Biblical Foundations of Marriage

Companionship, Unity, and Love

Companionship

"It is not good that man should be alone; I will make him a helper comparable to him."

Genesis 2:18

God created marriage as a relationship of companionship, mutual support, and unity.

The Unity of Marriage

"For this reason a man shall leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife... So then, they are no longer two but one flesh. Therefore what God has joined together, let not man separate."

Matthew 19:5-6

Christ teaches the sacred unity and permanence of marriage, calling spouses to lifelong faithfulness.

Sacrificial Love

"Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself for her."

Ephesians 5:25

Christian marriage is founded upon self-giving love modeled after Christ's sacrifice.

The Holiness of Marriage

"Marriage is honorable among all, and the bed undefiled."

Hebrews 13:4

Marriage is honorable and sacred, calling spouses to faithfulness, purity, and mutual respect.

Read Ephesians 5:21-33 as a whole: the Christian household is called to mutual reverence, love, and responsibility in Christ.

The Church Fathers on Marriage

St. John Chrysostom

In his teaching on Ephesians 5, Chrysostom calls spouses to shape family life around Christ. He emphasizes the husband's sacrificial love, the couple's mutual responsibility, and the formation of a household where Scripture, prayer, and Christian virtue are lived.

The Coptic Orthodox Crowning Ceremony

The Mystery of Crowning

The Sacrament of Holy Matrimony includes the beautiful and deeply symbolic Crowning Ceremony. During the marriage service, the priest places crowns upon the heads of the bride and groom.

The crowns express the dignity, responsibility, and spiritual calling of Christian marriage. The couple begins a shared life as a new Christian household, called to serve God and one another.

Royal Dignity

The crowns symbolize the honor and responsibility entrusted to the couple as they establish their household.

Sacrificial Love

The crowns remind the couple that Christian love requires self-denial, patience, forgiveness, and sacrifice.

A Life of Holiness

Both spouses pursue holiness together, supporting one another through prayer, faithfulness, and spiritual growth.

The Marriage Prayers

The service asks God to bless the couple with unity of heart, faithfulness, mutual love and respect, peace, spiritual strength, wisdom, and a household centered on Christ.

The priest guides the couple through the rite and its preparation according to the Church's practice and their circumstances.

Christ at the Center of Marriage

A Christ-Centered Household

Christian marriage flourishes when Christ remains at the center of the relationship. Husband and wife reflect His love through their daily lives.

Mutual Forgiveness

Marriage requires humility, patience, and a willingness to forgive.

Sacrificial Love

Each spouse places the good of the other above selfish desires.

Prayer Together

Shared prayer strengthens spiritual unity and helps the couple face difficulties with faith.

Growing in Virtue

Daily life offers opportunities to practice patience, kindness, self-control, faithfulness, and compassion.

Spiritual Unity

The couple grows together in communion with Christ and participation in the life of the Church.

The Family as a Domestic Church

Christian tradition understands the household as a place where faith is lived and passed from one generation to another. A Christian home is called to be a place of prayer, hospitality, forgiveness, service, and spiritual formation.

Spiritual Preparation for Marriage

Preparing for a Lifelong Covenant

Marriage is a sacred vocation requiring thoughtful spiritual preparation. Consult the priest early, so preparation can be guided by the Church's teaching and the couple's circumstances.

Premarital Counseling

Meet with the priest to discuss the spiritual responsibilities of marriage, Christian family life, communication, and preparation for the sacrament.

Confession and Repentance

Prepare through repentance, self-examination, and participation in the sacramental life of the Church.

Prayer and Spiritual Growth

Pastoral Guidance

Marriage as a Path to Holiness

Growing Together in Christ

Marriage is a vocation through which husband and wife grow in holiness. Daily responsibilities become opportunities to overcome selfishness and practice the sacrificial love revealed by Christ.

Virtues in Daily Life

  • Humility and patience.
  • Forgiveness and selflessness.
  • Faithfulness and generosity.
  • Prayer and service.

Raising Children in the Faith

Where God blesses a family with children, parents nurture them spiritually through:

  • Family prayer.
  • Participation in the Divine Liturgy.
  • Scripture reading.
  • A faithful Christian example.
  • Teaching the Orthodox faith.
  • Practicing love and service.

The holiness and dignity of marriage are not dependent upon a couple's ability to have children.

Intercultural Marriages and Unity in Faith

One Faith, Many Cultures

The Coptic Orthodox Church is rooted in Egypt but embraces faithful members from many cultural and ethnic backgrounds. Christian marriage is not restricted to couples who share the same ethnicity, nationality, or language.

Cultural diversity can enrich family life through different traditions, experiences, and expressions of hospitality. The essential foundation of Orthodox sacramental marriage is unity in the Orthodox Christian faith.

Couples from different cultural or religious backgrounds should speak with their priest about the Church's sacramental requirements, catechesis, and preparation.

Willingness to undergo catechesis does not by itself establish eligibility for an Orthodox marriage ceremony. Pastoral guidance and applicable Church requirements must be followed.
