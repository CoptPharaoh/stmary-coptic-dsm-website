FEATURED AUTHOR

H.H. Pope Shenouda III

117th Pope of Alexandria and Patriarch of the See of St. Mark (1971–2012)

His Holiness Pope Shenouda III was a beloved spiritual father, theologian, teacher, and author whose writings continue to guide generations of Orthodox Christians.

Through his books, sermons, and pastoral teachings, he addressed Christian spirituality, repentance, prayer, Scripture, theology, and the daily practice of the Orthodox faith.

His writings are known for presenting profound spiritual teachings in an accessible and practical manner.

A LIFE OF FAITH

Reading for Spiritual Growth

Spiritual reading has an important place in Orthodox Christian life. The writings of the Church's spiritual fathers and teachers help believers deepen their understanding of Holy Scripture, strengthen their prayer lives, and grow in repentance, humility, and love for Christ.

These resources are offered to encourage thoughtful study and spiritual growth. Reading should accompany participation in the sacramental and liturgical life of the Church.
